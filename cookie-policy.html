Cookie Policy

Effective: 22 April 2026 · Last updated: 15 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Digicreature Technologies ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit digicreaturetechnologies.moneymindtool.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — such as your language preference, login state, and browsing patterns — which improves your experience and helps us understand how the site is used.

Some cookies are set by us ("first-party cookies") and others are set by third-party services we use ("third-party cookies"), such as Google Analytics or embedded content providers.

2. Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time.

Category Purpose Examples Retention
Strictly necessary Enable core functionality like form submission, secure session handling, and basic navigation. Cannot be disabled. Session cookies, CSRF tokens Session — 1 hour
Functional Remember your preferences (e.g., accepted chatbot state, cookie banner dismissal, form drafts). Chat state, banner state Up to 12 months
Analytics Help us understand which pages visitors read, how they navigate, and where they drop off so we can improve the site. Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*) Up to 2 years
Marketing Measure the effectiveness of paid advertising campaigns and personalise ads on partner networks. Meta Pixel, Google Ads, LinkedIn Insight Tag Up to 13 months

3. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party services that may set their own cookies. Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how they process the data collected:

  • Google Analytics 4 — for aggregated visitor analytics. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Ads — for measuring paid campaign performance.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — for measuring social advertising campaigns.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — for measuring B2B campaign performance.
  • Google Fonts — for typography delivery. May log IP addresses in transit.

These services may transfer data outside India, including to the United States. Where such transfers occur, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and GDPR.

4. Your choices & how to manage cookies

You have several ways to control the cookies we use:

4.1 Cookie banner

When you first visit our site, our cookie banner lets you accept all, reject non-essential, or customise your choices. You can revisit your decision at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in our footer.

4.2 Browser controls

Most browsers let you delete existing cookies, block specific domains, or prompt you before storing new ones. Refer to your browser's help:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

4.3 Opt out of third-party tracking

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through:

  • Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Your Online Choices (EU)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US)

Note that blocking or disabling cookies may impact site functionality — for example, forms may not remember your entries, and analytics-driven improvements may take longer to reach you.

5. Do Not Track & global privacy controls

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal or Global Privacy Control (GPC). Where technically feasible and legally required, we honour these signals as a request to disable non-essential tracking. Our analytics stack respects consent signals sent via IAB TCF v2.2 or equivalent frameworks.

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the cookies we use, legal requirements, or best practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where required, notify you through our website or by email.
