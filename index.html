FAQ

Common
questions.

Still unsure? WhatsApp us and we'll help.

How much will this cost?+
Marketing engagements start at ₹25,000/month, e-commerce builds from ₹89,000, AI agent builds from ₹1,49,000, and data pipelines are scoped per project. Every quote is fixed-scope and itemised before we stores from ₹1,49,000. Every quote is fixed-scope and itemised before we start — no surprises.
How fast can you actually launch a site?+
Once the design is finalised and signed off, AI handles the build — most sites go from approved design to live in about 4 hours. Om Sakthi Catering Services went from nothing to designed, built and live in 6 hours end to end. The design and scoping conversation before that is the part that takes real time, and it is worth doing properly.
What does "AI-first" actually mean here?+
It means AI is in the delivery, not in the brochure. We use LLMs to draft content, parse documents, generate and review code, cluster keywords, and power the agents we ship to you. A senior engineer reviews everything before it goes live — you get the speed of AI with a person accountable for the result.
Which AI models do you build on?+
We're model-agnostic and pick per use case — frontier models where reasoning quality matters, smaller or open-weight models where cost and latency matter. You own the choice: the prompts, the evaluation set and the model configuration are handed over with the code, so you can switch providers without us.
What happens to our data?+
Your data stays yours. We run on API tiers that exclude your inputs from model training, keep retention to what the build needs, and can deploy inside your own cloud account where the data cannot leave your perimeter. NDAs and data-processing terms are standard — just ask.
How do you stop the AI getting things wrong?+
Every agent ships with an evaluation set, guardrails on what it is allowed to do, full logging of what it did, and a human-escalation path for anything low-confidence. We report accuracy against that evaluation set rather than asking you to take it on faith — and we will tell you when a task is a poor fit for AI.
Do I own the code & assets?+
Yes — 100%. Code, designs, accounts and content are all handed over to you. We don't lock you in.
What if I want to cancel?+
Monthly retainers are cancellable with 15 days' notice. Project work is milestone-based — pay only for what's delivered.
How quickly can we start?+
Most projects begin within a few business days after the discovery call and scope sign-off.
Where is your team based?+
India — with senior delivery for clients across India, UAE, UK, and US time zones.
Do you sign NDAs?+
Yes. Mutual NDAs and statements of work are standard. Just ask.
